Understanding Phishing Attacks

Phishing is one of the most common cybersecurity threats people encounter online. Attackers often send emails or messages that appear to come from trusted organizations to trick people into sharing sensitive information.

Common Warning Signs

  • Emails asking for passwords or personal information.
  • Messages creating a false sense of urgency.
  • Suspicious links or unexpected attachments.
  • Unusual sender addresses or spelling mistakes.

How to Protect Yourself

Always verify the sender before clicking links or downloading attachments. If a message seems suspicious, contact the organization directly using its official website or phone number.

Learning to recognize phishing attempts can help prevent identity theft, financial loss, and unauthorized access to your accounts.
